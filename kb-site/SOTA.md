# Current SOTA

The current state of the art as we practice it - one verdict per line. Statuses: `[sota]` = our current best answer, apply it by default; `[directional]` = believed but not proven, verify before betting on it; `[retired]` = tried and rejected, do not resurrect.

Maintenance: append new verdicts with a date; when a verdict changes, flip its status to `[retired]` in place and add the replacement - never silently delete. Each verdict links its evidence (skill file, playbook, PR, or external source).

## Idea Selection

- [directional] Problem-first: build for a pain we personally have, felt daily or weekly, with real heat, owned by a specific narrow group we can describe "to the point of discomfort". Behavior over stated preference - people say read-more, do scroll-til-2am. Evidence: rork-guide (third-party; vendored at `kb-site/external/rork-guide/`) GUIDE §4. (2026-08-17)
- [directional] Gotcha-moment test: ONE feature a 5-second sound-off viewer fully understands, returning a personalized answer only usage can produce. Spend ~half of build time on it and design that screen like a billboard - in a creator video nobody reads captions. Evidence: rork-guide (third-party; vendored at `kb-site/external/rork-guide/`) GUIDE §8, §11. (2026-08-17)
- [directional] Tarpit filter: the AI habit-tracker / journal / meal-planner / flashcards class (and any straight clone of five existing apps) competes on marketing only - the weakest axis for a small team vs funded studios. A clone is admissible only with a mechanism-level differentiator visible on screen; "better designed" is not a view. Evidence: rork-guide (third-party; vendored at `kb-site/external/rork-guide/`) GUIDE §5. (2026-08-17)
- [directional] Solve a solved problem with a DIFFERENT MECHANISM (3AK stride analysis inside Strava's market). Uniqueness is what makes distribution cheap: novel mechanism = point a camera at it; familiar mechanism = manufacture interest forever. Evidence: rork-guide (third-party; vendored at `kb-site/external/rork-guide/`) GUIDE §6. (2026-08-17)
- [directional] Reverse-engineer the viral video before building: find the format already winning with the audience, then build the product that belongs inside it (RizzGPT). If we cannot picture a specific creator showing it inside their format without it feeling like an ad, skip the idea. Evidence: rork-guide (third-party; vendored at `kb-site/external/rork-guide/`) GUIDE §7. (2026-08-17)
- [directional] Distribution amplifies the idea, never rescues it: same founder, same month - Wrestle AI 1M views -> $17k; clone dating app 1.8M views -> $35. Weak idea + reach = failing faster in front of more people. Evidence: rork-guide (third-party; vendored at `kb-site/external/rork-guide/`) GUIDE §6. (2026-08-17)
- [directional] Consumer validation is behavioral, not interviews: post the demo and watch whether strangers stop scrolling (a ~300-view TikTok floor is the test bench), paywall and watch payments, read competitors' 1-star reviews and niche creators' comment sections. Interviews are a B2B tool. Evidence: rork-guide (third-party; vendored at `kb-site/external/rork-guide/`) GUIDE §4, §14. (2026-08-17)
- [sota] Every new app idea gets roasted against `kb-site/IDEA-ROAST.md` (hard kills + soft flags, recorded verdict) before any build time - no recorded roast, no build. Evidence: IDEA-ROAST.md; owner request 2026-08-17. (2026-08-17)

- [sota] Lockout winback: dismissal routes to a discounted yearly offer ($14.99 `lockinchinese.pro.yearly.winback`); the word "winback" never appears in customer-facing copy. A/B by appending candidate ids. Evidence: PR #1035. (2026-07-30)
- [sota] Benchmarks we hold ourselves to: trial start 15%+, trial-to-paid 30%+, no-trial install-to-paid 4% floor. Placements before experiments. Evidence: paywall-experiments playbook (Superwall, 10k+ experiments). (2026-07-31)
- [sota] Transaction-abandonment discounts are ANNUAL ONLY - weekly/monthly discounts tested dead. Evidence: paywall-experiments playbook. (2026-07-31)
- [sota] Paywall copy mechanics: say "free" 5-7x, promise a trial-end reminder, hide trial length from the product card, trial-toggle dual pricing (~10% buy no-trial), prices behind the CTA, authentic numbers ($3.33) can beat .99. Evidence: paywall-experiments playbook. (2026-07-31)
- [sota] Weekly plan + 3-day trial delivers ~1.5x average LTV of other configs. Evidence: Adapty 2026 in CATALOG benchmarks. (2026-07-16)
- [sota] SDUI carries copy only; RevenueCat packages carry the plan mix. Never encode price/plan changes in SDUI. Evidence: lockin RC offering catalog decision. (2026-08-01)
- [sota] Never trust StoreKit `isEligibleForIntroOffer` alone - false negatives in TestFlight/sandbox even for fresh accounts. Resolve as store bit OR zero-transactions-in-group proof. Evidence: PR #1123. (2026-08-03)
- [sota] Honest paywall: clear trial timeline + "we'll remind you before you're charged" (+23% trial signups at Blinkist). Evidence: P9, CATALOG Blinkist entry. (2026-07-16)

- [directional] Trial attached to the ANNUAL plan only ($9.99/mo, $59.99/yr, 3-day trial on annual) steers buyers to annual and banks ~$60 upfront - cash timing funds the next creator buy. ## Motion

- [directional] Animation is communication, not decoration; the acceptance test is invisibility - users report "smooth", never "animated". Sterile and overdone are both failures. Evidence: motion-principles skill; distilled from [R. Salaja, 12 Principles of Animation](https://www.raphaelsalaja.com/library/12-principles-of-animation) (linked, not vendored - copyrighted). (2026-08-17)
- [directional] Timing defaults: under ~300ms for routine interactions, ~150ms for tooltips/hovers; define shared duration/spring tokens per app and reuse them. Evidence: motion-principles skill; distilled from [R. Salaja, 12 Principles of Animation](https://www.raphaelsalaja.com/library/12-principles-of-animation) (linked, not vendored - copyrighted). (2026-08-17)
- [directional] Frequency gates animation: surfaces used constantly in a session (context menus, tab switches, keyboards) animate minimally or not at all; anticipation cues are reserved for significant transitions. Evidence: motion-principles skill; distilled from [R. Salaja, 12 Principles of Animation](https://www.raphaelsalaja.com/library/12-principles-of-animation) (linked, not vendored - copyrighted). (2026-08-17)
- [directional] Staging: one focal motion at a time - choreograph backdrop -> surface -> primary control; never animate many peers simultaneously. Evidence: motion-principles skill; distilled from [R. Salaja, 12 Principles of Animation](https://www.raphaelsalaja.com/library/12-principles-of-animation) (linked, not vendored - copyrighted). (2026-08-17)
- [directional] Stagger list entrances per item but cap the total delay - accumulated stagger reads as lag, not life. Evidence: motion-principles skill; distilled from [R. Salaja, 12 Principles of Animation](https://www.raphaelsalaja.com/library/12-principles-of-animation) (linked, not vendored - copyrighted). (2026-08-17)
- [directional] Never linear for spatial motion; standardize a small easing set, prefer springs on Apple platforms (WWDC23 "Animate with Springs"). Arcs are showpiece-only in product UX. Evidence: motion-principles skill; distilled from [R. Salaja, 12 Principles of Animation](https://www.raphaelsalaja.com/library/12-principles-of-animation) (linked, not vendored - copyrighted). (2026-08-17)
- [directional] Exaggeration belongs to feedback moments only (error shake, confirmation, onboarding beats, empty states) - routine navigation never exaggerates. One secondary flourish max per action; pair with the sound cues kit (PR #1170). Evidence: motion-principles skill; distilled from [R. Salaja, 12 Principles of Animation](https://www.raphaelsalaja.com/library/12-principles-of-animation) (linked, not vendored - copyrighted). (2026-08-17)
- [sota] CLOCK RULE: inside TimelineView animate off `timeline.date`, never a captured `value.time` - mixed clocks skew phase between elements. Evidence: lockin motion lab (retired lab, verdict baked). (2026-08-17)
- [sota] Widgets and app extensions get system-driven transitions only - no custom animation loops. Evidence: extension discipline (analytics-per-app playbook, bundle-size split #1043). (2026-08-17)
- [sota] Pow (MIT, iOS 15+, macOS 12+, `github.com/EmergeTools/Pow`, 30 named effects: pop, pulse, vanish, poof, shake, glow, shine, jump, wipe, iris, ...) is a REFERENCE CATALOGUE for us, never a dependency inside an embedded SDK. A library third parties ship in their own App Store binary must not force a transitive animation package on them: SwiftPM resolves one version graph, so a host already on Pow gets version-pinned by us, and every host pays the binary size. Name the Pow effect in the spec, implement it natively. App-level targets may depend on it freely. Evidence: Pow README (MIT); bundle-size split #1043; ShakeToShip pen-cursor design 2026-08-24. (2026-08-24)
- [directional] Chrome that appears INSIDE a screen recording animates as little as possible. A recording overlay is not ordinary UI: every looping animation is re-encoded into the artifact a reviewer watches, spends bitrate on furniture, and pulls the eye off the thing being demonstrated. Transitions resolve under ~300ms then hold static; no infinite loops in a recorded overlay beyond a single low-amplitude status pulse. Evidence: ShakeToShip recording cursor (island lab, 2026-08-24); extends the "frequency gates animation" verdict above. (2026-08-24)
- [directional] `Shubham0812/SwiftUI-Animations` (Apache-2.0, iOS 17+, 26 self-contained scenes: scratch-to-reveal, text swirl, fluid grain, card swap, loaders) is a clone-and-study Xcode project, NOT a SwiftPM package. Use it to lift a technique into our own file; never vendor the project. Evidence: repo README. (2026-08-24)

Position weight is real - the earlier a term sits in the 30-char name, the more it counts, and almost nobody searches your brand until you are big. Brand-first is a conscious vanity trade, never a default. Evidence: [vibe-aso](https://github.com/Kronop/vibe-aso) (third-party, MIT; vendored at `kb-site/external/vibe-aso/`) keyword-research.md. (2026-08-15)
- [directional] Name, subtitle and keyword field are indexed as a combination: never repeat a *phrase* across the three surfaces. Shared words in genuinely distinct searches ("calorie tracker" vs "ai calorie tracker") are fine; when trimming, drop the duplicate from the keyword field, never from the name. Evidence: [vibe-aso](https://github.com/Kronop/vibe-aso) (third-party, MIT; vendored at `kb-site/external/vibe-aso/`) keyword-research.md. (2026-08-15)
- [directional] Difficulty gate: under ~50 is winnable for a new app, 50-70 becomes contestable only once the app has installs and ratings. A low-install app ranking ~1000 for a term in its own subtitle has an authority problem, not a keyword problem - more keywords will not fix it. Evidence: [vibe-aso](https://github.com/Kronop/vibe-aso) (third-party, MIT; vendored at `kb-site/external/vibe-aso/`) keyword-research.md. (2026-08-15)
- [directional] A popularity floor value (5 on a 5-100 scale) means "below the instrument's resolution", not "no traffic". Main and subtitle keyword must sit clearly above the floor; floor-value long-tails are filler only, after the proven terms are placed. Evidence: [vibe-aso](https://github.com/Kronop/vibe-aso) (third-party, MIT; vendored at `kb-site/external/vibe-aso/`) keyword-research.md. (2026-08-15)
- [directional] Two failure classes fake relevance because their popularity looks great: too-generic ("tracker") and adjacent-wrong-intent ("muscle growth" for a calorie app). Test every candidate with "how many people typing this want THIS app?" - "some, indirectly" means cut. Evidence: [vibe-aso](https://github.com/Kronop/vibe-aso) (third-party, MIT; vendored at `kb-site/external/vibe-aso/`) keyword-research.md. (2026-08-15)

## Paid UA

We run no paid UA today and no app is live, so every verdict here is `[directional]` by construction - adopted from a third-party pack, never tested by us. Re-rate them against our own numbers before betting a budget.

- [directional] Minimum viable daily spend is a floor, not a target: $50/day per social or UAC channel, $30/day for Apple Search Ads. Below the floor a channel never leaves the learning phase - pause it instead of starving it. Evidence: [ua-skills](https://github.com/appeeky/ua-skills) (third-party, unlicensed - linked not mirrored; notes at `kb-site/external/ua-skills/`) cross-channel-budget. (2026-08-24)
- [directional] At or under $1,500/month, run **one** channel. Splitting a small budget across ASA plus two social channels kills all three in learning. Two channels start near $3,000/month, three near $10,000/month, and only if each still clears its floor. Evidence: [ua-skills](https://github.com/appeeky/ua-skills) (third-party, unlicensed - linked not mirrored; notes at `kb-site/external/ua-skills/`) cross-channel-budget. (2026-08-24)
- [directional] Channel launch order for an iOS subscription app: ASA first (needs only a live listing, highest intent, no creative production, real attribution rather than SKAN delay), then one social channel once the MMP is verified, then the second social after 7 days of data. Evidence: [ua-skills](https://github.com/appeeky/ua-skills) (third-party, unlicensed - linked not mirrored; notes at `kb-site/external/ua-skills/`) cross-channel-budget. (2026-08-24)
- [directional] Attribution comes before spend: no paid social campaign runs until an MMP is installed and purchase/subscribe events are verified end to end. Which MMP matters far less than having one. Exactly one source fires purchase events - an SDK plus RevenueCat both firing is double-counting. Evidence: [ua-skills](https://github.com/appeeky/ua-skills) (third-party, unlicensed - linked not mirrored; notes at `kb-site/external/ua-skills/`) mmp-setup. (2026-08-24)
- [directional] Campaigns optimize toward the down-funnel event (Purchase, Subscribe, Start Trial), never installs and never a Traffic objective. Optimizing for installs buys installs that do not convert. Evidence: [ua-skills](https://github.com/appeeky/ua-skills) (third-party, unlicensed - linked not mirrored; notes at `kb-site/external/ua-skills/`) ua-skills README, meta/tiktok campaign setup. (2026-08-24)
- [directional] Every campaign an agent creates lands PAUSED and stays paused until a human activates it. Spend without verified tracking is the failure this prevents. Evidence: [ua-skills](https://github.com/appeeky/ua-skills) (third-party, unlicensed - linked not mirrored; notes at `kb-site/external/ua-skills/`) meta-campaign-setup, ad-creative-to-meta. (2026-08-24)
- [directional] Kill criteria stated before launch: pause a test at 2x target CPA with zero conversions after $100 spend; pause a channel at ROAS below 0.5x for 14 days. Hold 5-10% of budget as a test reserve, and reallocate on no less than 7 days of data. Evidence: [ua-skills](https://github.com/appeeky/ua-skills) (third-party, unlicensed - linked not mirrored; notes at `kb-site/external/ua-skills/`) cross-channel-budget. (2026-08-24)
- [directional] Cap any single channel at 50% of budget until it has produced 50+ conversions; scale a proven channel 20% per week and stop when CPA rises 15%. Evidence: [ua-skills](https://github.com/appeeky/ua-skills) (third-party, unlicensed - linked not mirrored; notes at `kb-site/external/ua-skills/`) cross-channel-budget. (2026-08-24)
- [directional] The ATT prompt fires after a value moment, never on first launch. This is an attribution rule, not only a UX one - a first-launch prompt costs the opt-in rate every paid channel depends on. Evidence: [ua-skills](https://github.com/appeeky/ua-skills) (third-party, unlicensed - linked not mirrored; notes at `kb-site/external/ua-skills/`) mmp-setup; consistent with our own permission-priming rule in Onboarding. (2026-08-24)
